## Megido72: a 10-million-character game project

According to the [first-chapter description](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV15spk6iEVR/), the author retranslated about 10 million characters with YN using GPT Sol 6.1 and Grok 4.7, replacing the latter with Sol 6.1 partway through. Naming consistency and accuracy improved over the earlier translation, and the demonstrated text had no manual edits; errors caused by omitted Japanese personal references still occur. [View the translated stories](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV12ypy68EEF/).
